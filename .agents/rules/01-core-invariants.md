# Core Invariants: House Rules and The Anti-Slop Purpose Filter

Build software and interfaces that look crafted by an experienced designer for a real client, not generated by an AI from a generic template. The benchmark quality bar is figma.com and x.ai.

---

## 1. The Anti-Slop Purpose Filter

Every visual decision, component, and animation must be held to the **Purpose Test**:
> **What does this technique serve?**
> If the only answer is *"it looks like modern tech"* or *"it's the AI default"*, the technique must be removed or redesigned. Techniques are kept only when they directly serve visual hierarchy, usability, content readability, or genuine brand identity.

### The Identity Test
> **If the logo and brand name were swapped out, would this interface still feel distinct, purposeful, and authentic to the product?**
> If the answer is no, the design is too generic. Ground it in the actual product material.

### The 5 Craftsmanship Standards
- **C-1 Intentionality:** Every color, stroke, font size, and spacing unit has a clear, articulable rationale.
- **C-2 Functional Completeness:** Every interactive control works. No dead buttons, fake links, or non-functional dropdowns.
- **C-3 Content-Driven Composition:** Sections exist because the product's actual content requires them, not to fill a generic landing page checklist.
- **C-4 Resilience:** The UI holds up cleanly in all states (empty, loading, active, error), all breakpoints, and keyboard-only navigation.
- **C-5 Evidence Over Claims:** Everything presented as fact (numbers, reviews, ratings, security badges) is real and verifiable. Never fabricate social proof or statistics.

---

## 2. The 9 House Rules

These nine rules take precedence over everything else:

1. **No captions under visuals:** Never place an explanatory caption, disclaimer, or label under any image, diagram, or render. License credits go into a single consolidated footer line.
2. **Every visual renders:** Never leave an empty ground, broken image, or grey skeleton box at 1440, 1024, 768, 390, and 320px.
3. **One section per screen:**
   - The hero section stands completely alone on the first screen.
   - Every subsequent section is budgeted to exactly one or two screens tall at 1440x900.
   - No neighbouring section's heading may peek into the active viewport.
4. **Equal content edges:** Content containers must maintain strictly symmetrical left and right margins aligned to grid bounds.
5. **Micro-animations and motion layer:**
   - Linked cards lift **2px** on hover (`hover:-translate-y-0.5`).
   - Photos zoom **1.02–1.03x** inside their clipping frame on hover (`group-hover:scale-[1.025]`).
   - Buttons step on hover and depress on click (`active:translate-y-0 active:scale-[0.98]`).
   - Underlines draw smoothly.
   - Card animation loops cycle in 2.5–4 seconds, pausing or resetting on hover.
   - **Strictly forbidden:** 3D tilt effects, automatic carousels, and timed auto-switching tabs.
   - Hero enters once on initial load (`data-enter`).
6. **Authentic photography leads:** Feature real, high-resolution photographs of products, spaces, and craftsmanship rather than generic stock imagery or illustrations.
7. **Real full-colour icons:** Always use official, recognized product/tool SVGs in their true brand colors, never hand-drawn approximations or generic icon sets.
8. **Trade-restricted visual effects:**
   - Shader effects, 3D scenes, and WebGL loops belong ONLY on businesses with technical subjects (tech, AI, developer tools, gaming, fintech, hardware).
   - Physical places, cafes, bakeries, guesthouses, and retail shops must be led by authentic photographs and clean, flat color.
9. **Address and contact privacy:**
   - Street and district only (e.g. *Jl. AR Hakim, Ditotrunan, Lumajang*).
   - No house numbers, postcodes, or raw coordinates.
   - No company register or VAT numbers.
   - Demo contact forms use `.example`.
